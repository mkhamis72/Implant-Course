# Schedule

Feature rules for this folder, split out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (its conventions still apply). Numbers in brackets are migrations in `web/supabase/migrations/`; the SQL is the source of truth.

- `implant_sites.technique` is `guided` / `free_hand`, nullable in the DB (older rows and the previous app version) but required by the app's Zod schemas; the schedule colours bookings by it (`bookingTechnique`). Bookings are edited in place with `update_surgery_slot` (same day only) and completed/reopened with `set_slot_status` (0027). Bookings need a surgery session that day: Schedule editors add one from the Schedule page ("Add surgery day") through `add_surgery_session`, which needs Edit on Schedule and only creates `surgery` sessions (0039); other session types are still added under Courses (Edit on Courses).
- **Placed by (0114–0116):** the app books with `create_surgery_slot_v2` / `update_surgery_slot_v2` and checks clashes with `slot_conflicts_v2` (a doctor booked twice clashes), every parameter named; the old names stay for the previous app version. A course organizer chooses `Placed by` Delegate (default: placing delegates, supervisor required) or Doctor (no placing delegates, assisting allowed, `Supervisor (optional)`); the shared radio is `src/components/placed-by-choice.tsx`. A clinic (`teachingOn` false) books a doctor only: the form hides course, delegates and supervisor, and `bookSurgery` clears them whatever the client sent. Editing a booking made before the switch keeps what it recorded (0120): `updateBooking` re-reads `supervisor_id` / `placing_doctor_id` and sends the recorded supervisor with no delegates (empty = keep; `update_surgery_slot_v2` refuses only a change); a booking placed by its recorded delegates (`placedByRecordedDelegates`: supervisor, no doctor) takes no doctor (schema `placedBy: recorded`, set only by the action). Cards show `Doctor: <name>` where delegates appear.
- **Clinic booking (0122):** a clinic has no surgery-day list or `Add clinic day`; the page offers `Book surgery` with Date and Where (`surgery_slots.setting`: In clinic / GA in hospital). The app calls only `create_surgery_slot_v3` / `update_surgery_slot_v3` (`p_setting`, `p_date`): in a clinic `p_date` picks the day and `app_private.clinic_day(date)` (advisory lock) reuses the earliest course-less session or creates a hidden `Clinic day` 00:00–23:59, and editing the date moves the booking; for an organizer both are null. The doctor defaults to the signed-in user's linked doctor (`defaultDoctorId`). v2 stays for the previous app version. Lectures and workshops are teaching-only: the tabs are hidden and `_talks/guard.ts` `requireTalks()` sends a clinic to `/schedule`.
- **Day grid hours:** the Schedule grid shows 07:00–20:00 and stretches to the full hours that hold that day's live bookings (`gridRange`, `src/lib/schedule/time.ts`); cancelled bookings and session hours don't stretch it.
- **Lectures & workshops (0028):** `sessions.type` lecture/workshop sessions hold `talks` (time, `lecturers` row, topic), written only via `create_talk` / `update_talk` / `delete_talk`, which refuse times outside the session, overlaps in the session and double-booked lecturers. UI: `schedule/lectures|workshops`, sharing `schedule/_talks/`. Sessions have an optional `room`. Display: a timetable (`_talks/day-view.tsx`): day buttons for the days with sessions of that kind within 14 days (`dayButtons`), each session a `<details>` bar (`session-block.tsx`; open by default: the one running now, else the next today, else the first, `openSessionId`), talks as time → lecturer → topic. Breaks are derived, not stored: a gap of 10 minutes or more between consecutive talks (`talkTimeline`, `src/lib/schedule/timeline.ts`). The room is not shown (one hall per course); the column stays.

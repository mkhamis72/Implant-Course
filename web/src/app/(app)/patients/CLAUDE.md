# Patients

Feature rules for this folder, split out of the root `CLAUDE.md` (its conventions still apply). Numbers in brackets are migrations in `web/supabase/migrations/`; the SQL is the source of truth.

- **Implant picker** (Plan / Place forms, `patients/[id]/implant-picker.tsx` + `src/lib/inventory/implant-picker.ts`):
  - The steps are brand (only when several brands are active), line, type, then size.
  - Line and type are `products.system` split at `" · "` ("PSK-MDK · PSK Micro"). New catalogue rows must keep that format; a system without `" · "` is a line with no type step.
  - "In stock only" counts stock usable on the placement date (`availableLots`).
- **Inventory off:** when the plan lacks Inventory (`ChartData.inventoryOn` false) placement and components are catalogue-only (no batch step, no "In stock only" filter), and the corrections panel offers "Correct product" / "Attach stock batch".
- **Receiving stock while placing:** with no usable stock, the placement form receives it in place through `receiveStockForPlacement`. That action needs Edit on Inventory (`ChartData.canReceive`) and doesn't redirect, so the typed fields survive the refresh.
- **Patient list filter:** `search_patients(q, include_archived, stages, course, supervisor)` (0098). `stages` is one array of implant stages (`implant_stage`), chart states `missing` / `extracted` (`tooth_status`) and the scan filters `digital_scan` / `conventional` / `impression_pending`, ORed (0041, 0054); options in `src/lib/patients/stages.ts` (`prosthetic` exists in SQL but isn't offered). `course` / `supervisor` (null = all) match through a placed implant or a booking that isn't cancelled (same rule as `patient_course_options`). On the page they are selects (courses from `course_options()`, supervisors including inactive ones, `src/lib/patients/list-filters.ts`) and the stage boxes sit in compact `FilterMenu` dropdowns (`filter-menu.tsx`). Don't add an overload: 0098 dropped the 3-argument function first. The page lists pages of 50 (0124): `search_patients_v2(…, p_offset, p_limit)` (by name then id, limit capped at 100) and `search_patients_count(…)` share the filter in `patient_list_matches`; `?page=` (past the end = last page) and the pager links (`pager.tsx`, `src/lib/patients/pages.ts`) keep every filter, and a new search starts at page 1. `search_patients` stays for the previous app version.
- **Placement and course reports (0035).**
  - Placement took a required course with `place_implant_v3` (0060; now `place_implant_v4`, below): the old placement function plus a required `p_course` (the Place form's Course select, running courses first; `correct_placement_v3` does the same for Corrections). `place_implant_v2` and `correct_placement` were dropped in 0063. The Place form doesn't capture torque, ISQ or bone type: they are recorded in Surgical details through `save_implant_surgery_v2` (0053; 0055 skips the implant update when they are unchanged). The old `save_implant_surgery` was dropped in 0056.
  - The old 13-argument `place_implant` was dropped in 0052. Don't add an overload of a function with defaulted parameters: PostgREST's named-argument calls become ambiguous.
  - `course_report(course)` is security invoker, so implants show only to users with Patients or Schedule view. The report's summaries and CSV are built in `src/lib/reports/course-report.ts`.
- **Placed by a delegate or a doctor (0115, modules release 3).** The app now calls `place_implant_v4` / `correct_placement_v3` (v3 / v2 plus `p_placing_doctor`; the old ones were dropped in 0131). A course organizer chooses `Placed by` Delegate (default; supervisor required) or Doctor (`ChartData.doctors`, supervisor optional, assisting delegate and course kept); a booking with a doctor starts on Doctor. The schemas (`placedBy`) send only the chosen person. In a clinic (`ChartData.teachingOn` false) the Place, Correct, Component and Restoration forms, site details, the Implants table, the consent print page and the patient list show no course, delegate or supervisor fields: only a required doctor. Hidden is kept (0119): what a record named before the company became a clinic goes back unchanged (the `correctPlacement` / `updateRestoration` actions re-read it server-side in a clinic and ignore what the client sent), and `correct_placement_v3` / `update_restoration` in a clinic refuse only a change (clearing included). With Doctor chosen the supervisor starts empty. In a clinic an implant a delegate placed takes no doctor (the Correct form hides the field and `correct_placement_v3` refuses one, 0121). With a course organizer, an implant placed while a clinic keeps its null course in Corrections.
- **Implant entered by mistake (0099).** `remove_placement(site, reason)` (Corrections tab → "Remove implant", a second click confirms) erases a placed, restored or failed implant as if it was never entered: its stock movements (and its components') are deleted so every batch is back where it was, the site row goes with its children by cascade, its study code is deleted and the tooth becomes `missing`. It refuses while the implant is in a restoration that isn't voided. The reason is written to `implant_sites.removal_reason` just before the delete, so the delete's audit row (History: "Implant on tooth N removed (entered by mistake): reason") holds who, when, why and what; that UPDATE is hidden from History (`isHiddenFromHistory`). Re-entering is a normal placement. `delete_patient` deletes a duplicate patient with implants the same way.
- **Enrolled delegates (0081).** A delegate on a placement, restoration or component must be enrolled in that record's course: `app_private.check_enrolled` in `place_implant_v4`, `correct_placement_v3`, `add_restoration`, `update_restoration` and `fit_component_v3` (0082 dropped the old `fit_component`), and the pickers use `delegatesForCourse` (`src/lib/inventory/enrolled.ts`). Corrections and restoration edits check only a delegate or course that changes, so older records stay editable; a recorded delegate outside the list is shown as "(not enrolled)". No course on a restoration or component lists all active delegates; the Place form requires a course first. Components have their own `course_id` (empty on older records), defaulting to the implant's. Patients view can read `enrollments`.
- **Component roles (0133–0138).** `app_private.component_role` gives each fitting a role: healing, abutment, cap, abutment_screw, prosthetic_screw, transfer (scan body, impression coping) or legacy. One current row per (site, role) (`site_components_one_current_role`). The app calls only `fit_component_v3` (`fit_component_v2` dropped in 0144; DB tests make rows it left behind, such as a legacy `final_restoration`, with `legacyFit` in `tests/db/fixtures.ts`). Healing and abutment exclude each other; fitting a healing or abutment part also closes a current legacy `final_restoration` row; when an abutment closes (any healing or abutment fitting), the parts on it close too: its abutment screw, prosthetic screws holding it and its cap. A healing cap (role cap, 0138) is a cap on a multi-unit abutment: it needs `holds_component_id` = the current abutment (`Choose the abutment this cap sits on.`) and replaces only the current cap; the Component form asks "Sits on", the panel shows "on <abutment>". Screws hold `holds_component_id` (the current abutment) or `holds_restoration_id` (a live restoration on that implant); `torque_ncm` is recorded per fitting. `replaced_by` links a closed row to what replaced it, and `void_component` reopens exactly those rows. Transfer rows are stored already closed and can be undone (stock returns). Undo refuses with `Undo the part that sits on this one first.` (a current screw or cap on it) or `Undo the newer part in the same place first.` 0136 backfilled `replaced_by` for older rows; 0137 cleared links whose closer's date did not match (once; its function went in 0144). UI: the Component form groups parts by role (specs line, torque, Holds); the implant panel shows Current parts plus history with "replaced on"; Corrections has a "Component to undo" select (current parts, a current old `final_restoration` row, transfers). History hides the update that only closes a part (the `replaced_by` update after it reads "Component replaced") and shows a reopened part as "Component back in place".
- **Restorations (0057–0061).** `restorations` (stage `temporary`/`final`; type single crown / bridge / overdenture / full arch; teeth covered; fitted date; retention, material, delegate, supervisor, course, notes; end: `ended_on`, `end_kind` `replaced`/`remade`, `end_reason`), `restoration_sites` (the implants it sits on) and `restoration_problems` (chipping, fracture, debonding, screw loosening/fracture, wear, other, with action taken). Written only through `add_restoration` / `update_restoration` / `void_restoration` and `record|update|void_restoration_problem` (optimistic on `updated_at`). Rules are in SQL and mirrored for the form in `src/lib/restorations/`: a single crown has one implant, the others at least two; all implants placed, the patient's and in one jaw; teeth covered are one contiguous run in arch order that includes every implant tooth (cantilevers extend it); fitted date today or earlier and not before an implant's `placed_on`. Adding one ends every current restoration on its implants (`replaced` for a temporary; a final ended by a final is `remade` and needs a reason); a temporary over a final is refused (record a problem instead). The database owns `implant_sites.state = restored`: an implant is restored exactly while it has a current, non-voided final restoration, and the tooth status follows. Loading (immediate ≤ 7 days after placement, early ≤ 2 months, else conventional) is derived in TS only (`LOADING_IMMEDIATE_DAYS`, `LOADING_EARLY_MONTHS`). Problems belong to the restoration; the implant complication form no longer offers the crown types (`screw_loosening`, `screw_fracture`, `restoration_fracture`, `loss_of_retention`, `prosthetic_remake`) though SQL still accepts them. 0059 converted existing `final_restoration` components into restoration records (existing rows only, nothing seeded). Readers: `course_restorations(course)` (course report table and the per-delegate Restored count), `my_restorations()` and `delegate_restorations(delegate)` (delegate reports; a restoration's course is `course_id`, else the course containing `fitted_on` where its delegate is enrolled), and `course_options()` (0061: courses for the pickers, View on Patients). UI: `patients/[id]/restorations.tsx` (Restoration tab), odontogram bars ("Bridge 34–36"; the first tap on a covered tooth opens its restoration, the second selects the tooth).
- **Scan / impression (0054).** `implant_sites.impression_type` (digital scan / conventional) and `impression_taken_on` are written only through `record_impression` (optimistic on `updated_at`); empty means not taken. UI: `ImpressionControl` on the Implant tab (`patients/[id]/impression-control.tsx`), labels in `src/lib/implants/impression.ts`; patient-list filter values are in the Patient list filter bullet.
- **Research capture (0043–0044).** Medical-history risk factors are yes / no / not assessed (`null`): `smoking`, `diabetes_status`, `*_status`, `periodontitis_history`, `bruxism`, `previous_implant_failure`, plus `hba1c`/`hba1c_date`, `cigarettes_per_day` (`src/lib/patients/risk-factors.ts`). The old booleans (`diabetes`, `smoker`, …) were dropped in 0050; `medical_history_check` refuses a future HbA1c date. History labels for the old columns stay in `src/lib/patients/history.ts` for older entries. Placed implants have one `implant_surgery` row (timing, extraction date, phenotype, mucosa, flap, torque type, healing, closure, grafting performed) and any number of `implant_grafts`, written through `save_implant_surgery_v2` (0053; optimistic on `updated_at`; it also records insertion torque, ISQ and bone type on `implant_sites`, so Surgical details completeness counts 12 fields) / `add|update|delete_implant_graft`; rules and labels in `src/lib/implants/surgery.ts`, UI in `patients/[id]/surgical-details.tsx`. An empty value means not recorded — never treat it as "no". Roadmap of later research parts: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-17-research-capture-surgery-design.md`.
- **Follow-up visits (0045–0046).** `implant_followups` rows are visits: scheduled (`visit_key`: `early_review`, `osseointegration`, `month_6`, `month_12`, `year_N`) or unscheduled (null), with `attendance` and findings (pain, swelling/suppuration/plaque/mobility, four-site probing and bleeding, recession (facial/mesial/distal, mm, 0076), ISQ, prosthesis, radiograph). Recorded only through `record_visit` (findings as one jsonb, validated in SQL; not editable — void and re-record). The schedule is `visit_schedule()`; status (upcoming / due / overdue / missed / done / did not attend / not needed) is calculated only in SQL by `implant_visits(patient)` and `recall_list()` (security invoker, View on Patients) — never recompute it in TS. Rules in `src/lib/visits/`, UI in `patients/[id]/visits.tsx`, recall page `/patients/recall`. `record_followup` was dropped in 0051; failures recorded through it have no details until `record_failure` adds them (DB tests recreate such rows with `legacyFailure`). `app_private` functions can't be called from security-invoker functions (no schema usage for `authenticated`).
- **Radiographs (0064–0065, 0080).** Coverage (Specific teeth / Upper arch / Lower arch / Full mouth; a panorama presets Full mouth) isn't stored: the form fills `teeth` and `coverageOf` works it out when shown. `radiographs` (date, type `periapical`/`panoramic`/`cbct`, purpose `planning`/`baseline`/`loading`/`follow_up`/`other`, teeth, optional link and notes) and `radiograph_bone_levels` (one row per implant: `mesial_mm`, `distal_mm`) are written only through `save_radiograph` (optimistic on `radiographs.updated_at`) and `void_radiograph` (reason required; voided rows hidden everywhere); `radiographs_for(patient)` reads them. `save_radiograph` replaces the whole measurement set, so the form must send every implant's values or the omitted ones are deleted (the form refuses to untick a measured tooth); since 0065 it upserts, deleting only implants no longer measured and updating only changed values, so an edit adds no History noise. Rules: date today or earlier (before placement only for a planning X-ray; "other" since 0075); 1+ valid FDI teeth, no duplicates; baseline / loading / follow-up need an implant placed by the date on one of the teeth; a level can only be on a placed / restored / failed implant on one of the X-ray's teeth, placed on or before the X-ray's date (0065); levels are -5.0 to 15.0 mm, positive = bone below the shoulder, null = not measured (never 0), each row needs mesial or distal; link must be http(s) and opens in a new tab. UI: patient "X-rays" tab (`patients/[id]/xrays/`, Add / Edit / Remove) and the "X-rays" tab in an implant's tooth panel (`implant-xrays.tsx`, reuses `XrayCard` with `onlySite`, so edits still carry every implant's levels); labels and rules in `src/lib/radiographs/`; History labels in `src/lib/patients/history.ts`.
- **Dates at entry (0075).** Date pickers offer only possible dates: implant records from the placement to today (restoration fitted from the latest placement of its ticked implants; a restoration problem from its fitting; a non-planning X-ray from the first placement on its teeth; extraction no later than placement), patient dates up to today; forms with `ChartData` use `data.today`. SQL stays the authority: 0075 refuses an "other" X-ray before the first placement on its teeth (baseline / loading / follow-up already needed an implant placed by then) and `correct_placement_v3` can no longer move the placement after its scan or a non-planning X-ray of its tooth taken since it was placed.
- **Outcomes and data quality (0066–0068).** The outcome readers feed the Statistics page (0069); `data_quality_issues` has no UI since 2026-10-24 (impossible dates are refused at entry, see Dates at entry; prod had none), kept for one-off checks. All are security invoker with View on Patients: `outcomes_version()` (`'outcomes-v1'`), `icoi_grade(...)` (the ICOI cut-offs are constants inside it and nowhere else; owner rule: mobility or pain ≥ 4 grades IV and, with pain/mobility/suppuration recorded, a III sign grades III without an X-ray — only a possible I/II needs bone loss), `visit_grades`, `implant_outcomes` (survival means still in the mouth: `lost` / `removed` are events, `failed_in_place` survives; censored at the latest contact, milestones at loading / 1 year / year N: `failed` / `survived` / `not_reached` (milestone still in the future) / `unknown` (lost to follow-up); owner rule: a legacy failure with no `failed_on` is dated by its earliest failed visit, and only without one is it `event_time_unknown`; `latest_grade_reason` names what set the latest grade), `survival_at`, `crown_outcomes` (event = `end_kind = 'remade'`; implant events dated the same way) and `data_quality_issues(patient)` (impossible dates only: a visit, complication, restoration, non-planning X-ray or `failed_on` before placement, any of those in the future, a restoration problem before its fitting). Status is decided only in SQL. 
- **Consent (0072).** `consent_form_versions` (label, `effective_on`, `text_en`, `text_ar`; seeded v1 from `docs/consent/consent-form-v1.md`), `patient_consents` (version, language, `signed_on`, answers `treatment` / `research` / `teaching_images` / `follow_up`, `file_id`, void with reason) and `stored_files` (bytes; closed to every role, no audit trigger: records audit the file id only). Written through `record_consent` / `void_consent` (Edit on Patients) and `save_consent_version` (admin, Admin → Settings → Consent). A version is locked once any record references it (`first_signed_at`); the current one is the latest `effective_on` ≤ today. Current consent = latest non-voided by `signed_on`, then `created_at` (`patient_consents_for`). Scans are served by `consent_file` (View on Patients) via `GET /patients/[id]/consent/file/[fileId]` (inline, `no-store`, 404 otherwise). Files: JPEG or PDF, 1 B–2 MB, magic bytes checked in TS (`src/lib/consent/files.ts`) and SQL; photos re-encoded to JPEG ≤ 2000 px in the browser; uploads need `bodySizeLimit: '3mb'`. Printing: `/patients/[id]/consent/print` (prefilled) and `/admin/settings/consent/print` (blank), text filled and escaped by `src/lib/consent/render.ts` (unknown placeholder = `__________`). The patient header shows "No consent on file"; the Statistics page warns about patients lacking research consent and the export's `patients` table has `research_consent`. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-governance-design.md`. In a clinic the tick-box labels come from `consentItems(teaching)` (`src/lib/consent/labels.ts`) and `New version from the clinic template` (`?template=clinic`, `src/lib/consent/clinic-template.ts`, marked `Draft — needs review`) is offered; the template needs legal and Arabic review before any real patient signs it.
- **Complications and failure (0047–0048).** `implant_complications` (category → type, noticed/resolved dates, severity, treatment, optional visit link) are written through `record_complication` / `update_complication` (optimistic on `updated_at`) / `void_complication` (details as jsonb, validated in SQL); open = no resolved date; open ones mark the tooth on the odontogram. Failure lives on `implant_sites` (`failed_on`, `failure_type`, `failure_reason`, `failure_notes`): a "failed" visit must carry the details (`record_visit` → `app_private.apply_failure`), old failures get them through `record_failure`, edits through `correct_failure` (optimistic on `implant_sites.updated_at`); voiding the failed visit clears them. Early vs late is derived in TS (`src/lib/implants/failure.ts`), never stored. Visits of a failed implant stop at the failure date. Peri-implantitis/mucositis are clinician-recorded; the form only shows the 2017 case definitions.
- **Implant types (0139–0141, 0144).** A product carries `implant_kind` (`conventional` / `zygomatic` / `pterygoid`, default conventional; library-locked like the specs). `implant_sites` has `implant_type` (null while planned; `place_implant_v4` sets it from the product's kind, `correct_batch` re-defaults it only while it still equals the old product's kind), `planned_product_id` (the plan survives placement, a spare keeps the primary plan; the app shows "Planned: …" when it differs), `angulation_deg` 0–60, `angulation_direction`, `anchorage`, `entry_point`, `trajectory` (≤ 200). Geometry is written only by `set_implant_geometry` (Edit on Patients, placed/restored only, also in a clinic): tilted needs an angle, a direction needs an angle (`Give the angle as well as the direction.`, 0144; older rows untouched); anchorage, entry point and trajectory only for zygomatic/pterygoid. UI: Surgical details "Implant type and angle" (own Save), implant panel type line and "Emergence N", chart ZYG/TLT/PTG tag, Implants tab Type column. `planned_product_id` has its FK `implant_sites_planned_product_id_fkey` (company_id, planned_product_id) → products again (0141 dropped it for one release, 0144 re-added it): with two implant_sites→products keys every embed of products from implant_sites must name `products!implant_sites_product_id_fkey` (an unhinted one fails as ambiguous, PGRST201). The app still loads planned products by id (`withPlannedProducts`, `chart-data.ts`).
- **Full-arch view (C1, no new data).** The chart has a "Teeth | Full arch" switch (two `aria-pressed` buttons in `chart-panel.tsx`; `?view=arch` opens Full arch, the switch updates the URL with `history.replaceState`, no server round trip). Full arch draws `FullArch` (`src/components/full-arch/`) from `fullArchModel({ sites, spans, badges, types })`, built from `ChartData` like the tooth chart: each implant under its emergence tooth (conventional upright, tilted at its recorded angle (for conventional and tilted the recorded direction names where the head tilts, so the apex goes the other way: an All-on-4 "30° distal" converges on the midline), zygomatic long and pterygoid angled with the apex distal unless mesial is recorded; planned as an outline, typed from the planned product, upright; spares not drawn), ZYG / TLT / PTG and component tags, each current restoration as one connected bridge (temporary dashed, pontic units lighter) with links from its implants. Implants and bridges call the same `onSelect` as the tooth chart (a bridge opens its first implant on the Restoration tab), the selection survives the switch and the tooth panel below is unchanged. It is labelled schematic, not a substitute for CBCT or 3D planning. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-full-arch-design.md`.
- **Full-arch cases (0142–0143, C2).** `full_arch_cases` (patient, arch `upper` = quadrants 1–2 / `lower` = 3–4, configuration `all_on_4` / `all_on_6` / `all_on_x` / `other`, name ≤ 60, notes, `closed_at` + `close_reason`) and `full_arch_stages` (one row per case and stage `scan` / `lab_design` / `try_in`; `done_on`, `lab` only on lab and design, `passive_fit` and `occlusion` only on the try-in, notes); `implant_sites.case_id` and `restorations.case_id` link a case's implants and bridges. Company pattern of `doctors`; read like restorations (View on Patients); only the definers write. Rules: one open case per patient and arch (`full_arch_cases_one_open`, "This arch already has an open full-arch case."); an implant is in at most one case and must be the patient's, on the arch and not removed, but an implant in a closed case can be taken by a new one (it leaves the closed case); a restoration joins a case only with every tooth on its arch. The `restoration_sites_case_link` trigger (`app_private.link_restoration_to_case`) puts a new restoration in the open case of its implants when all its teeth are on that arch. A case bridge is a linked, non-voided restoration of type bridge, full arch or overdenture (a single crown on a case implant stays linked but never counts); `caseBridges` gives the Temporary (the latest temporary bridge, even after a final replaced it, with its fitted date) and the current finals. Delivery is not a stage: the case is delivered once its current final bridges together cover every placed or restored implant of the case (failed ones are not needed; with none placed never), on the latest fitted date among them; finals that leave implants out show "Fitted <date> · covers 12, 22 of 15, 12, 22, 25". Stage dates run from the case's first placement to `cairo_today()`, and a stage needs a placed implant in the case. Status is derived in TS only (`caseStatus`, `src/lib/full-arch/cases.ts`): Closed, else Completed when the current final bridges cover every placed or restored implant (above), else In treatment with a placed / restored / failed implant, else Planned. Functions (Edit on Patients, optimistic on `full_arch_cases.updated_at` except stages and restoration links): `create_full_arch_case`, `update_full_arch_case`, `set_case_sites`, `set_case_restoration` (null unlinks), `record_case_stage` (creates or replaces), `clear_case_stage`, `close_full_arch_case` (reason required), `reopen_full_arch_case`; actions in `patients/[id]/case-actions.ts`. History (`patient_history` reads both tables): "Full-arch case started / updated / closed / reopened / deleted", "Full-arch stage recorded / updated / cleared", and a restoration's `case_id`-only update reads "Linked to full-arch case" / "Removed from full-arch case". UI: under the Full arch drawing, one panel per arch (`patients/[id]/full-arch-case.tsx`): "Start upper/lower case" (planned and placed implants pre-ticked), Edit case, Change implants, Temporary / Final bridge lines, the three stages with Record / Edit / Clear and Delivery, Close case / Reopen; the drawing shows a caption per arch ("Upper arch · All-on-4 · In treatment", "Scan ✓ · Lab — · Try-in — · Delivery —"); the Restoration tab shows "Part of: …" with Link to case / Unlink from case.
- Tooth state `extracted` is labelled "To be extracted" (the tooth is still present); the enum value is unchanged.
